# The Website Mill — Eight-Site Elite Review

Date: 16 August 2026  
Branch: `agent/seven-site-showcase`  
Review model: UX/customer experience, marketing/social, growth/business development, founder/key decision-maker, and implementation review.

## Scoring method

Each concept is reviewed separately on mobile and desktop for clarity, visual distinction, conversion journey, interaction quality, credibility and responsive behaviour. A portfolio-demo score measures the quality of the demonstrated design and journey. Launch readiness is lower where real photography, verified business details, credentials, policies or reviews have not yet been supplied.

The target test widths are 360, 390, 430, 768 and 1440 pixels. Reconstructed Before states are comparison concepts, not archived screenshots.

## Panel-wide decisions

- One audience, one visual system and one primary commercial action per site.
- No fabricated reviews, qualifications, locations, outcomes, availability or project histories.
- Internal notes and stock-image caveats belong in the Website Mill evidence layer, not in the customer journey.
- Mobile headings, gaps and fixed actions must be deliberately controlled.
- Every interactive control must work or be removed.
- The eight acquisition models remain different: Reiki booking; landscaping site visit; art collector enquiry; friendly cleaning estimate; retail checkout; nail appointment; membership subscription; premium cleaning tailored quote.

## 1. LuxeBeorn

### Baseline panel score

- Desktop: 4.5–6.5/10
- Mobile: 3.5–4.5/10

### Main failures found

- Mixed centred and left-aligned mobile composition.
- Excessively large headings, blank space and repeated reassurance sections.
- Public references to the children and the meaning behind their names.
- Flowery treatment names and repeated poetic language.
- Inconsistent typographic tone and too many competing sections.

### Implemented

- Rebuilt from scratch around one left-aligned mobile content rail.
- Removed every child-name, name-origin, light/warrior and family-story reference.
- Replaced the hero with a concise distance-Reiki proposition and one CTA.
- Renamed treatments plainly: 30-minute session, 60-minute session and four-session plan.
- Made all three durations, purposes and prices visible without a tab interaction.
- Reduced the page to hero, sessions, three-step process, practitioner/boundary, FAQ and booking.
- Collapsed FAQs by default and simplified the availability request.
- Standardised typography to one serif display family and one sans-serif body family.
- Added strict mobile spacing and alignment overrides.

### Post-build target

- Portfolio demo: 9.2/10 desktop, 9.4/10 mobile.
- Launch readiness depends on an approved practitioner portrait, biography, credentials and genuine client feedback.
